Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, and what we never do with it.

Nova Refrigeration & Appliance Repair is a residential appliance repair company serving the Austin, Texas metro. This policy explains what happens to the information you give us when you call, text or use the service request form on this site. It was last updated on 8 September 2026.

What we collect

When you submit the service request form we receive the name, phone number, email address, city, appliance type, brand and problem description you enter. When you call or text us we have your phone number and whatever you tell us about the repair. That is the entire set — we do not ask for payment details through this website, and the site does not process payments.

Why we collect it

Solely to schedule and carry out your repair: to call you back, confirm an appointment window, bring the right parts and follow up on the work. We may contact you about that specific request by phone, text or email.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list because you requested a repair.
  • We do not share your details with third parties except the service providers below.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to our email inbox through Web3Forms, which processes the submission and forwards it to us. The service area maps on this site are embedded from Google Maps, and Google may set cookies when a map loads. We do not run advertising trackers or sell data to advertising networks.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores one thing in your browser: your light or dark mode preference, kept in local storage on your own device so the site looks the same on your next visit. It is not an identifier, it never leaves your browser, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

Service requests stay in our email and job records for as long as we need them for warranty, follow-up repairs and standard business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

Ask us to correct or delete what we hold, or ask us to stop contacting you, and we will — call (512) 740-0408 or email info@novarefrigerationappliance.com. If you would rather not submit anything through this website at all, call or text us instead; nothing is required through the form.

Children

This site is meant for homeowners arranging appliance repair and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Call or text (512) 740-0408, or email info@novarefrigerationappliance.com.
