Dishwasher Repair

Dishes not getting clean or water pooling at the bottom? We fix it.

From clogged pumps to failed control boards and leaks, we restore your dishwasher to spotless, leak-free performance.

Common Dishwasher problems we fix

  • Not cleaning properly
  • Not draining
  • Water leaks
  • Control board replacement
  • Pump replacement

Cleaning and draining diagnosis

Poor cleaning can begin with a blocked filter or spray arm, low water fill, weak circulation, or a heating problem. Standing water at the end of a cycle leads us to inspect the drain path and pump. We separate these symptoms so the repair addresses the actual cause.

Useful details for a service request

Share the model number, whether water remains in the tub, whether the dishes are wet or dirty, and where any leak appears. If the dishwasher is leaking onto the floor, stop running it and turn off the water supply when safe. Photos of the leak or an error code can also help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is water left in my dishwasher after a cycle?
A clogged filter or drain line, a failed pump, or a control fault may prevent draining. We inspect the drain system before replacing parts.
Can you repair a dishwasher that leaks?
Yes. We trace leaks to their source, which may be a door seal, hose, pump, or another component.
Do you service integrated dishwashers?
Yes. We service integrated and panel-ready dishwashers as well as standard models.
