Ice Maker Repair

No ice, slow ice, or an overflowing ice maker? We get it producing again.

From built-in ice makers to refrigerator dispensers, we repair the assemblies, valves, and controls that keep the ice flowing.

Common Ice Maker problems we fix

  • No ice production
  • Slow ice production
  • Overflowing ice maker
  • Ice dispenser issues
  • Water valve replacement
  • Ice maker assembly replacement

Why ice production slows or stops

Ice-making depends on water supply, freezer temperature, the fill path, and a working harvest cycle. A clogged filter or frozen fill tube can resemble a failed ice-maker assembly. We check each part of the sequence so a water-flow problem is not mistaken for a new ice maker.

Signs worth reporting

Tell us whether the unit makes no ice, produces small cubes, leaks, overfills, or has a dispenser that will not release ice. The model number and any recent filter change are useful too. The pattern helps narrow the diagnosis and identify whether the issue is in the ice maker, water system, or freezer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why has my ice maker stopped making ice?
Common causes include a blocked water supply, a failed inlet valve, a frozen fill tube, or an ice-maker assembly fault. We diagnose the cause before replacing parts.
Can you fix an ice maker that makes ice slowly?
Yes. We check water flow, temperature, filters, and the ice-maker cycle to find what is limiting production.
Do you repair built-in ice makers?
Yes. We service built-in ice makers as well as ice makers inside refrigerators.
