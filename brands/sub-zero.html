Common Sub-Zero issues we diagnose

Built-in refrigeration calls for careful diagnosis and respect for the surrounding cabinetry. Nova investigates Sub-Zero cooling, frost, water, and sealed-system concerns in Austin-area homes.

  • Built-in refrigerator temperature problems
  • Freezer frost and airflow
  • Water leaks and ice production
  • Sealed-system diagnosis

Built-in refrigerator diagnosis

Sub-Zero units are often installed within cabinetry, so ventilation, door sealing, temperature readings and access all matter. We document the symptom and inspect cooling components before discussing a repair.

Freezer, ice and sealed-system work

A frost pattern, slow ice production and a warm compartment point to different systems. We check the relevant airflow, water and refrigeration components and explain the scope of any sealed-system work before it begins.
