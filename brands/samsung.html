Refrigerator and ice-maker diagnosis

On a Samsung refrigerator, note whether the fresh-food section, freezer, or both are warm. Ice production, water flow, frost and fan noise offer different clues. We compare those symptoms with the model information before recommending a part.

Laundry and kitchen appliance service

Samsung washer, dryer, dishwasher and cooking-appliance problems need different test paths. Tell us the model number, displayed code and exact point in the cycle when the problem occurs so the visit starts with the right checks.
