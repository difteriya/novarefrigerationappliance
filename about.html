About Nova Refrigeration & Appliance Repair

Trusted residential appliance and refrigeration repair serving Austin, Texas and surrounding communities.

Nova Refrigeration & Appliance Repair is a residential appliance repair and refrigeration company based in Austin, Texas. We help homeowners across the metro keep their kitchens and laundry rooms running — quickly, honestly, and affordably.

Refrigeration is our specialty

While we repair every major home appliance, refrigeration is where we go deepest. Our technicians handle sealed-system work that many shops won't touch — compressor replacement, refrigerant leak diagnosis and repair, filter drier replacement, system evacuation and recharge, capillary tube restrictions, and full sealed-system rebuilds.

We also have specialized experience with high-end and built-in refrigeration, including Sub-Zero, Thermador, Viking, Bosch, and GE Monogram.

Why homeowners choose Nova

  • Same-day service available across the Austin, Texas metro.
  • Up-front pricing with a clear estimate before any work begins.
  • All major brands — from Samsung and LG to Sub-Zero and Wolf.
  • Honest diagnostics — we'll tell you when a repair makes sense and when it doesn't.

Whether your refrigerator stopped cooling overnight or your dryer just won't heat, we're ready to help. Call us, text us, or request service online.
